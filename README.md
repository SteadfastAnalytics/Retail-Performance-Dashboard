# Retail Performance Dashboard

![Retail Performance Dashboard](images/retail-dashboard.png)

## The Business

A retail operation selling across multiple product categories — apparel, home goods, electronics, beauty, accessories — needed a single place to see whether the business was actually healthy month to month, not just whether sales were happening.

## What's On It

- **Top-line KPIs** — average order value, profit margin %, total orders, total profit, and total revenue, all at a glance
- **Revenue by month** — a clean trend line showing the real shape of the business over time, not just a snapshot
- **Revenue by category** — a breakdown showing exactly which product categories are carrying the business (Beauty, Apparel, Home Goods, Electronics, Accessories)
- **Revenue by product** — drilling down further, to the individual product level

## The Question It Answers

*Which products and categories are actually driving the business — and is revenue trending up or down?*

A retail owner can look at this dashboard for 30 seconds and know whether last month was good, which category to lean into, and which specific products are worth featuring or discounting.

## Built With

Power BI, connected directly to the store's point-of-sale and order data — refreshed automatically, no manual exports.
